TICKET-016
Status: planned

Problem:
- In a 3-4 person room nobody can tell who is talking: the tiles are identical and the
  only feedback is "you are talking" on your own button. People constantly ask
  "who was that?".

Requested changes:
1. Detect the speaking level of the local mic and of every remote stream with a WebAudio
   AnalyserNode and light up the corresponding tile (green glow + "speaking" badge).
2. Show "🎙️ <name> is talking" in the room header.
3. Everything must degrade silently: no WebAudio support (or a blocked AudioContext)
   must not break the room - the feature is simply switched off.

Plan:
- Backup to backups/backup016/ (index.html + README.md).
- Add analyser helpers + a small polling loop that updates the tiles.
- Add self-test checks: the speaking elements exist and nothing throws without WebAudio.
- Update ticket status to done.
