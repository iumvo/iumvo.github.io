TICKET-014
Status: planned

Problem:
- Push-to-talk can get stuck open: if the user holds the button (or the Space bar) and
  then switches tab/window/app, no pointerup/keyup reaches the page and the microphone
  stays LIVE - the user keeps broadcasting to the whole room without noticing.
- On touch devices a long press can also raise the context menu.

Requested changes:
1. Release the mic on window blur, on visibilitychange (tab hidden) and on
   pointercancel/lostpointercapture.
2. Use pointer capture so "release to mute" works even if the finger slides off the button.
3. Block the context menu on the PTT button and forbid text selection on it.

Plan:
- Backup to backups/backup014/ (index.html + README.md).
- Rework the PTT wiring (pointer capture + blur/visibility safety).
- Add self-test checks: the mic is muted again after a simulated blur.
- Update ticket status to done.
