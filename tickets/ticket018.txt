Problem:
- The user repeatedly has to guess what to do next: "send this text to them, then wait,
  then paste their answer...". The README explains it, but the app itself does not.
  In a 4 person room with 3 peers there are 3 different steps to track.

Requested changes:
1. Add a "What to do next" line/panel in the room that is derived from the current state:
   - nobody connected -> "make an offer and send it to someone"
   - waiting for an answer -> "send your offer to X and paste their answer below"
   - waiting for them -> "send your answer to X"
   - all connected -> "everyone is connected - hold the button to talk"
